Social Deduction Game is a fully offline assistant for moderating Werewolf, Mafia, and other social deduction games.

<b>Prepare games</b>
<ul>
<li>Manage custom rule sets, teams, roles, and statuses</li>
<li>Prepare players and the seating order</li>
<li>Assign roles randomly, from a selected set, or manually</li>
</ul>

<b>Run games</b>
<ul>
<li>Keep track of the seating circle, roles, teams, and player status</li>
<li>Show each player their role in a dedicated full-screen view</li>
<li>Work through night actions in the correct order</li>
<li>Manage day, night, life, death, and temporary statuses</li>
</ul>

Games, templates, and rule sets can be saved, loaded, imported, and exported. Library backups make it easy to move your data to another device.

All game and library data stays locally on the device. The app requires no account and contains no advertising or tracking.
