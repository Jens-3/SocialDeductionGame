# Privacy Policy for Social Deduction Game

[Deutsch](../de/privacy-policy.md) | English

This privacy policy applies to the Android app **Social Deduction Game**
(package name `io.github.jens_3.socialdeductiongame`).

## 1. Controller and contact

The controller responsible for the app is:

**Jens Aßmus**  
Email: **SocialDeductionGame@web.de**

## 2. Overview

Social Deduction Game is designed as an offline app. The app:

- does not require registration and has no user accounts;
- contains no advertising, analytics or tracking services;
- does not transmit personal data to the developer or to a server operated by the developer; and
- does not have permission to access the internet.

Game data and settings are processed exclusively on the device. Data is only
transferred when users initiate an export or sharing action and select a destination.

## 3. Data processed locally

To provide the app's features, the following content in particular may be
stored and processed locally on the device:

- game, scenario and rule data;
- freely entered labels, particularly player names;
- roles, teams, status information and game histories;
- imported or user-selected images and other game files; and
- app settings such as language, appearance and interaction preferences.

The app reads the language setting provided by the operating system to select
an appropriate interface language. This information stays on the device. The
listed data is needed to perform the functions requested by users. The developer
has no access to this locally stored content.

Users are advised not to enter confidential information or full real names in
free-text fields unless necessary for the game.

## 4. Device features and permissions

The app may use the following device features:

- **Vibration and haptic feedback:** to provide feedback during interaction;
- **screen orientation and keeping the screen awake:** for display during a game;
- **local app storage:** to store games, scenarios, settings, images and import and export files;
- **the operating system's sharing feature:** only following a deliberate user action.

The Android app does not request internet permission. Android cloud backups and
transfer of app data when switching devices are disabled for the app.

## 5. Import, export and sharing

Users can import data into the app and export game or scenario data. When
sharing, the app creates a temporary local export file and passes it through
the Android share menu to the app or person selected by the user.

Only this deliberate selection may cause data to leave the local app storage
area. Further processing by the selected recipient app or recipient is governed
by their respective privacy policies. The developer of Social Deduction Game
does not receive the shared data.

Exported files stored or shared outside app storage are not automatically
removed when app data is deleted.

## 6. Recipients and third parties

The app does not integrate advertising, analytics or tracking services and
does not transmit app data to the developer. Data is shared only at the user's
request through the sharing feature described in section 5.

Downloading and updating the app through Google Play are separate processes.
As the operator of the store and Android platform, Google may process data
independently. For details, see
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en).

### Hosting this privacy policy on GitHub Pages

When this webpage is published through GitHub Pages, GitHub logs and stores
visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. For further information, see the
[GitHub Pages documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection)
and [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
This processing concerns visits to the webpage. The Android app itself does
not transmit data to GitHub.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Locally stored app data generally remains until individual items are deleted
using the available app features. All local app data can also be removed via
**“Clear storage”** in Android system settings (the wording may vary by device)
or by uninstalling the app.

As the developer does not receive a copy of this data, the developer cannot
view, centrally correct, export or delete it. Exports stored outside the app
and copies already shared must be deleted separately at their storage location
or by the recipient.

## 8. Data security

Data is stored locally in the storage area designated for the app on the
Android device. The app prevents Android cloud backups and automatic data
transfer to a new device. Protecting the device itself, for example through a
screen lock, operating system updates and careful handling of export files, is
the user's responsibility.

## 9. Privacy rights and inquiries

Where the legal requirements are met, rights include access, rectification,
erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as
the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority.

As no personal app data is transmitted to the developer, the developer cannot
technically fulfil requests concerning content stored exclusively on the
device. General privacy inquiries can be sent to the contact address given in section 1.

## 10. Changes to this privacy policy

This privacy policy will be updated if the app's features, data processing or
legal requirements change.
